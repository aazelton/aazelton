About

Clinical capability and resilience for environments where infrastructure cannot be assumed.

Arcane Knowledge Group works at the intersection of clinical practice and applied technology — advising on and building systems for prehospital, austere, and resource-limited medicine. Our work spans AI clinical decision support, advanced clinical program development, and the operational realities of delivering care where connectivity, equipment, and backup cannot be taken for granted.

Every recommendation we make is grounded in current field practice. The principals hold active clinical credentials and test what they advise on — in rescue environments, on deployments, and in live EMS systems.

Always validated in the field, never in the conference room.

Team

Andrew Azelton

MSc, BS, NRP, FP-C, WP-C

Andrew is an austere and operational medicine specialist with more than twenty-five years of experience in prehospital critical care, rescue medicine, and high-risk medical operations. He has been building technology since he was fourteen — an early product of the open internet, hacker culture, and the habit of taking systems apart to understand how they hold together. That foundation in software and systems architecture now runs underneath the clinical work.

He holds a Master of Science in Austere Critical Care from the College of Remote and Offshore Medicine Foundation and is board certified as both a Flight Paramedic and Wilderness Paramedic. His career spans two decades in a high-volume urban EMS system, including special operations, clinical program development, and department-wide education leadership. His MSc thesis research examines artificial intelligence in austere and conflict-affected environments. He founded the AI in Austere Medicine Project, an open-source research initiative, and serves on the Wilderness Medical Society's Artificial Intelligence Committee as an Edge AI Architect. He is one of the few people working in this space who is equally at home writing the architecture and running the call.

Kendra Azelton

MSN, BSN, RN, CEN, LP

Kendra is a trauma clinical data analyst and nurse educator with more than twenty years in emergency care — a career that started on the ambulance as an EMT in 2004 and a paramedic in 2005, then moved to the emergency department, where she spent a decade as an ER nurse in a major health system. She holds a Master of Science in Nursing in nursing education, has been a Certified Emergency Nurse since 2015, and is dual-licensed in Texas as both a registered nurse and a licensed paramedic.

She currently serves as a Clinical Data Analyst for Trauma Services at a Level I academic trauma center, following a role as its trauma educator. Her work sits where AKG's does: in the space between clinical practice and the data systems that measure it — registry quality, performance improvement, and turning trauma data into decisions that change care.
